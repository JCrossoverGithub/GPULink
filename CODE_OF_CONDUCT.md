# Code of Conduct

GPUlink welcomes constructive participation from people of all backgrounds.
Be respectful, discuss technical disagreements in good faith, and focus review
comments on the work rather than the person.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, and the
publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.
Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to
protect a productive and safe project environment.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner rather than opening
a public issue.
